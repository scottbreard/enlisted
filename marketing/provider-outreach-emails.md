## Voice & rules for all provider marketing

- **Answer the exec-count question before they ask it.** Evasion reads as weakness; the
  honest "Sept 1 by design + known audience of 13,000+" story reads as strategy.
- **Sell the retention loop, not "traffic."** Compliance deadlines, renewals, and stock
  data are recurring reasons to log in that generic directories can't claim.
- **Offer the real number, always.** "Reply and we'll tell you exactly how registration
  is tracking" converts skeptics and costs nothing.
- **Never invent or inflate registration figures.** Cite the outreach database (13,000+),
  directory size (700+ firms), and verification process — all defensible.
- **Scarcity only where it's real:** 5 Featured spots per category, and the Sept 1 date.
